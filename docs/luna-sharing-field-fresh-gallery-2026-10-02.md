## Defects exposed and changes

- The first Luna Stage 3 read correctly dated the three offsite performances. Later formatting shifted October 2 Ocean Kin to October 9, October 3 Ocean Kin to October 3 with a weekly pattern, and October 3 Gorgon's Tale to October 4 with a weekly pattern. The existing dated-slot guard only recognized a month/day inside the clock evidence; this flyer put the date in an adjacent image cell. The guard now also recognizes a Stage 4 source weekday that agrees with its ISO date, provided the source is a nonrecurring schedule item with an explicit start and no recurrence language. It restores the source date and one-off status when Stage 5 shifts them. A focused regression reproduces the Gorgon's Tale error; genuine weekly events remain recurring.
- In r2, an untimed Friday/Saturday festival parent gained 4 PM from a linked page covering October 2–4. That clock belongs to a child performance, not every festival day. Website enrichment now declines to use a multi-day range candidate to fill a per-day item; a fixture confirms it leaves the parent untimed while exact-dated venue-site enrichment still works.
- The third full dry run, after both changes, returned the four source-worthy listings on the printed dates, the full five-act Sunday timetable in the parent description, correct images, and no recurrence. This one run demonstrates the current path, while the two focused regressions establish the specific guard behavior. It is not evidence of zero model variation across future posts.
