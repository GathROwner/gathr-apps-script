## Bounded inventory audit prototype

An opt-in audit now compares the Stage 2 estimated item count with the Stage 3 count of dated, timed calendar items. When the estimate exceeds that count by at least two, it reads at most two whole-image frames that Stage 1 marked dense, retains each frame's source gallery index, and reconciles literal date/title/time listings against the primary Stage 3 items. A read failure cannot fall back to text-only generation. With the default `applyAdditions: false`, it reports exact matches, possible omissions, conflicts, and unresolved dates without changing Stage 3 items or Firestore payloads. This is not an automatic completeness guarantee: Stage 2 counts can be wrong, and a one-item omission or a schedule classified outside `CALENDAR` may not trigger it.

Replaying the saved 13-item production result through the new diagnostic with the two preprocessed schedule images yielded 15 inventory listings: 13 matches, two possible additions, zero conflicts, and zero unresolved dates. Both additions came from gallery image index 2: `ENTERTAINMENT` at 2:00–4:00 pm with HACHE, and `CORNBOIL & COMMUNITY BBQ` at 2:00 pm with no printed end. The primary 13 were unchanged. Two additional Luna high Responses calls used 11,364 input and 4,894 output tokens, approximately $0.00358 at standard rates. The read-only report is `artifacts/cornfest-google-chat-2026-10-01/cornfest-inventory-trigger-replay.json`.
