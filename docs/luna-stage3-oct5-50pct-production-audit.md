## Decision and repair sequence

This run demonstrates that Luna is being exercised on useful posts, and the gallery provenance/recurrence guards worked on the selected examples. It is **not** clean enough to expand past 50%: two wrong-date cases comprising five live records, several lost source-grounded activities, and retry allocation that makes the exact cohort unstable. The 50% deployment was left unchanged during this read-only audit, following Craig's direction to let a large parse finish and not stop a canary for a single issue.
