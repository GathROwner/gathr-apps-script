## Rollout decision

The library canary supports the gallery and exact-post write path for a non-calendar, three-image post. It does not justify switching the scheduled parser. Next, enforce distinct source-backed seating times when a linked organizer page specifies them, and make duplicate matching preserve separately timed classes on the same date and venue. Re-run the Clamdiggers and museum rows through production selected-row dry tasks, inspect the predicted target document IDs, and only then consider a larger Luna allowlist. Treat image repetition and the market category as separate quality defects; do not hide the time and duplicate failures inside an event-count score.
